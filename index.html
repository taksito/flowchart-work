FLOWCHART DRAWING TOOL

Flowchart Drawing Tool流程圖繪製工具

A simple online tool for drawing flowcharts. Pick a shape, drop it on the canvas, connect the steps with arrows, and save your work. No installing, no signing in. 這是一個線上流程圖繪製工具：選好圖形、放到畫布上、用箭頭把步驟接起來，就能存檔。不用安裝，也不用登入。

  • Standard shapes 標準流程圖符號START / END, INPUT / OUTPUT, PROCESS, DECISION, LOOP
  • Drag to connect 拖曳接線Lines follow the shapes when you move them. 移動圖形時，線會自動跟著走。
  • Save and share 存檔與分享Download a .json work file or a PNG picture. 可以下載作業檔或 PNG 圖片。

How to hand in 繳交作業

  1. Type your name, then press “下載作業檔” to save a .json file. 填好座號姓名，按「下載作業檔」存成 .json 檔。
  2. Upload the file where your teacher tells you. 把檔案交到老師指定的地方。
  3. To keep working later, use “開啟檔案”. 下次要接著畫，用「開啟檔案」。
Your drawings are saved only in this browser. 作品只存在這台電腦的瀏覽器裡，換電腦前請先下載作業檔。
